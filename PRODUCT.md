# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: a static single-page site with no server runtime, forms, comments, or user-submitted content, chosen for cPanel compatibility and minimal attack surface.

## Users

Listeners discovering eklipse and existing listeners looking for a visual route into the project catalog.

## Product Purpose

Present eklipse as a Romanian underground electronic music project and guide visitors to the official Bandcamp project and album pages. The first viewport leads into the latest release, `Introspection I (remastered edition)`, before opening into the full catalog.

## Positioning

eklipse combines ambient layers and syncopated electronic beats with piano, cello, and moments of rave intensity, drawing on a catalog begun in Iași in 2001.

## Operating Context

The site is a single scrolling promotional experience at eklipse.ro. Visitors learn about the project, browse every release, and continue to Bandcamp to listen or purchase. Current and remastered editions lead; the three original versions remain visible in a clearly labeled archive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One responsive single-page experience with a space theme.
- English is the sole interface and metadata language.
- A short project description and a section for every Bandcamp album.
- No comments, forms, authentication, analytics, user input, or server-side application code.
- The only outbound links are the official eklipse Bandcamp project page and official eklipse Bandcamp album pages.
- Production is hosted from `/home/eklipse/public_html` on cPanel.

## Brand Commitments

- Artist name: eklipse. Always lowercase.
- Location and origin: currently based in Brașov, Romania; started in Iași, Romania, in 2001.
- Space is the binding visual subject.
- Album artwork from the official Bandcamp catalog is primary visual evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official project page: https://eklipse-music.bandcamp.com/
- Official catalog: https://eklipse-music.bandcamp.com/music
- Eleven official releases and their artwork, dates, descriptions, and album URLs are available on Bandcamp.
- No testimonials, press quotes, audience metrics, or performance claims were supplied; the site must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Let the music and original album artwork lead.
- Keep the route from discovery to Bandcamp obvious.
- Preserve a read-only, low-attack-surface implementation.
- Use factual copy grounded in the official catalog.
- Make the catalog legible and atmospheric on both mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus, sufficient contrast, meaningful image alternatives, and reduced-motion support.
